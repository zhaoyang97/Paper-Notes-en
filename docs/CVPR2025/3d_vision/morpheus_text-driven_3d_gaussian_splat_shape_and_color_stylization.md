# Morpheus: Text-Driven 3D Gaussian Splat Shape and Color Stylization

**Conference**: CVPR 2025  
**arXiv**: [2503.02009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02009)  
**Code**: [Project Page](https://nianticlabs.github.io/morpheus/)  
**Area**: 3D Vision  
**Keywords**: 3D Gaussian Splatting, stylization, RGBD diffusion model, shape editing, multi-view consistency, ControlNet

## TL;DR

Morpheus is proposed, an autoregressive 3DGS stylization method. Its core contributions include: (1) a novel RGBD diffusion model that achieves independent intensity control for appearance and shape stylization; (2) Warp ControlNet to propagate styles using warped composite frames; and (3) depth-guided feature sharing to ensure multi-view consistency.

## Background & Motivation

Reimagining real-world 3D scenes in different styles (e.g., "turning a living room into a Japanese tea room") is a compelling application scenario, which can also be used to expand the training distribution for downstream tasks. While new-view synthesis (NVS) based on 3DGS has developed rapidly in recent years, stylizing these 3D reconstructions remains a novel challenge.

**Key Challenge**: Existing NVS stylization methods mainly focus on texture/color editing and **cannot convincingly alter geometric shapes**. The reason is that any geometric change requires greater stylization intensity, but increasing strength destroys multi-view consistency. Existing 2D diffusion models lack explicit geometric understanding, the ability to output modified geometry, and independent control over appearance and shape stylization strengths.

Iterative methods like Instruct-NeRF2NeRF are time-consuming and yield blurry results, while methods like GaussCtrl share features through cross-attention but are prone to transmitting incorrect information under complex geometry, resulting in inconsistency. The goal of Morpheus is to achieve "controllable geometry + appearance stylization" and "multi-view consistency".

## Method

### Overall Architecture

The pipeline of Morpheus is as follows: (1) Input a depth-regularized 3DGS model, and render RGB and depth map sequences along a representative trajectory. (2) Autoregressively stylize each frame using an RGBD diffusion model for stylization, a Warp ControlNet to propagate the style of previous frames, and depth-guided feature sharing to maintain consistency. (3) Retrain a new 3DGS model using the stylized RGBD frames.

### Key Designs

**1. RGBD Diffusion Model (Independent Control of Shape and Appearance Stylization Intensity)**

Based on Stable Diffusion 2.1, the model is extended to an RGBD model: RGB and depth maps are encoded separately into the latent space, and the input channels of the U-Net are expanded to $4 \times 2 = 8$ feature channels + 2 temporal mask channels. The key innovation is **decoupled noise scheduling**—introducing independent maximum timesteps $T_{max}^I$ and $T_{max}^D$ for color and depth. During the denoising process, depth is not modified when $t > T_{max}^D$, and color is not modified when $t > T_{max}^I$, with masks informing the network which channel modifications are accepted. This makes it possible to "keep the color style constant while independently adjusting the degree of shape change."

**2. Warp ControlNet (Consistency Propagation)**

For each new frame, the previous stylized frames are forward-warped to the current view using depth and combined with the unstylized current frame to form a composite. A custom ControlNet is trained with the composite (RGBD + validity mask) as conditions to guide the RGBD diffusion model to: (a) correct artifacts in the warped regions; (b) fill in newly exposed regions in a manner consistent with the already warped regions. The hint network channels are expanded to (48, 96, 192, 384) to better understand the composition and stylization tasks. The training data consists of 250k pairs generated by the RGBD model itself.

**3. Depth-Guided Feature Sharing**

In the self-attention layer of the diffusion model, the query of the current frame is concatenated with the key/value of the reference frame to perform cross-attention. Key improvement: Construct a 4D heatmap $L_{ij}$ by forward-warping the depth of the reference frame to determine "which pixel in the reference frame corresponds to which pixel in the current frame," and only enhance cross-attention weights at corresponding positions (rather than globally uniform attention) to prevent aesthetic features from being wrongly copied to unrelated locations. Feature injection also uses the argmax of the same heatmap to select which reference feature to inject.

### Loss & Training

The 3DGS optimization employs a scale-invariant depth loss (compared with the median-scaled version of the Metric3D predicted depth), a normal orientation loss (dot product + cosine similarity), and TV L1 regularization to reduce floaters. Finally, the stylized 3DGS retraining utilizes both stylized colors and depths as supervision.

## Key Experimental Results

### Main Results: Quantitative Comparison (Table 1, 53 Stylization Groups)

| Method | CLIP Dir. Sim.↑ | CLIP Dir. Consist.↑ | RMSE↓ | LPIPS↓ |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Instruct-N2N | 0.098 | 0.531 | 0.0463 | 0.0540 |
| Instruct-GS2GS | 0.097 | 0.519 | 0.0501 | 0.0403 |
| GaussCtrl | 0.123 | 0.590 | 0.0471 | 0.0438 |
| DGE | 0.113 | 0.565 | 0.0384 | 0.0407 |
| **Morpheus** | **0.175** | **0.606** | **0.0370** | **0.0378** |

Morpheus leads across all metrics. The CLIP Direction Similarity reaches up to 0.175 (second place is 0.123), showing that the alignment of the style with the prompt far exceeds other methods. The processing time is around 10 minutes, on par with GaussCtrl and DGE.

### Ablation Study (Table 2)

| Configuration | Similarity↑ | Consistency↑ | Seq. RMSE↓ | Seq. LPIPS↓ |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Single-frame independent stylization | 0.161 | 0.592 | 0.1170 | 0.0941 |
| w/o Warp ControlNet | 0.170 | 0.604 | 0.0834 | 0.0917 |
| w/o Feature Sharing | 0.178 | 0.611 | 0.0817 | 0.0931 |
| Global Cross-Attention (w/o Depth Guidance) | 0.180 | 0.610 | 0.0730 | 0.0914 |
| **Full Model** | **0.175** | **0.606** | **0.0702** | **0.0911** |

- When Warp ControlNet is removed, the Sequential RMSE increases from 0.0702 → 0.0834, indicating its significant contribution to inter-frame consistency.
- Depth-guided attention vs. global attention: Prevents mismatching replication of aesthetic elements (see the bear eye case in Fig. 5).

### Key Findings

- **Shape stylization is a unique advantage**: By adjusting $T_{max}^D$, significant geometric changes can be generated while maintaining texture consistency (Fig. 3).
- User study (31 participants, 49 groups): Substantially outperforms all baselines in both "style prompt alignment" and "aesthetic quality" dimensions.
- ConsistDreamer can only perform local texture modification, whereas Morpheus enables scene-level structural transformation.

## Highlights & Insights

1. **Decoupled control is the core breakthrough**: The independent color/depth noise scheduling enables "controllable geometric stylization" for the first time—prior methods either did not modify geometry or became inconsistent when doing so.
2. **Depth-guided attention**: More precise than the epipolar line approach of GaussCtrl, as it directly utilizes 3D geometric information to determine pixel correspondences.
3. **Autoregressive vs. global optimization**: Compared to the iterative optimization of Instruct-NeRF2NeRF (which takes hours), the autoregressive pipeline completes in 10 minutes with better quality.

## Limitations & Future Work

- The stylization quality relies on the quality of the original 3DGS model; errors in the original splat (such as inaccurate depth or blurry regions) will be inherited by the stylized results.
- The autoregressive pipeline is sensitive to trajectory selection, requiring smooth camera trajectories to ensure sufficient inter-frame overlap.
- The RGBD diffusion model predicts scale-invariant depth, which needs to be scaled using rendered depth, potentially introducing additional errors.
- Limited by a $512 \times 512$ resolution, requiring further adaptation for high-resolution scenes.

## Related Work & Insights

- **GaussCtrl**: Shares features via cross-attention but does not consider geometry, leading to imprecise feature propagation.
- **Instruct-NeRF2NeRF/GS2GS**: Iterative optimization is time-consuming and prone to producing blurs.
- **ConsistDreamer**: Utilizes 3D noise representation but can only perform surface texture modifications.
- **Insights**: The decoupled control concept of the RGBD diffusion model can be extended to other 3D editing tasks; the training data generation method of Warp ControlNet (using its own model to generate paired data) is worth adopting.

## Rating

⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Clever method design (decoupled control + depth-guided attention), solving the long-standing limitation in 3D stylization of "not being able to alter geometry." The engineering implementation from Niantic Labs is also very solid.
