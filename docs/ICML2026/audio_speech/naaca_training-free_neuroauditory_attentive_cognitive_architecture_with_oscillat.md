<h1 data-number="4"><span class="header-section-number">4</span> Conclusion</h1>
<p>We presented NAACA, a training-free neurocomputational framework for auditorily salient event detection and attention gating. By biologically mapping audio spectrograms into a 2D damped wave field (OWM) and monitoring the resulting phase-coherence-driven energy transients, NAACA identifies critical information segments without any supervision. On the competitive XD-Violence benchmark, NAACA achieved the highest performance (70.6% AP) among all audio-only models (including supervised one) and reduced AudioQwen’s inference calls by 40%. Our analyses suggest NAACA aligns with cortical oscillatory mechanisms for maintenance and encoding. This research provides a computationally efficient path towards deploying massive LLMs on long-range, real-time sensing data.</p>
