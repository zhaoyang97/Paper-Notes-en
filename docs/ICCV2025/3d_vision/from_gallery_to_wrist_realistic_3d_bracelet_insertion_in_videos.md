# From Gallery to Wrist: Realistic 3D Bracelet Insertion in Videos

**Conference**: ICCV 2025
**arXiv**: [2507.20331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20331)
**Code**: [https://cjeen.github.io/BraceletPaper/](https://cjeen.github.io/BraceletPaper/)
**Area**: 3D Vision
**Keywords**: video object insertion, 3D Gaussian splatting, diffusion models, illumination enhancement, temporal consistency, virtual try-on

## TL;DR

This paper proposes a hybrid pipeline for realistically inserting 3D bracelets into videos. It leverages 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) to ensure temporal consistency, employs a 2D diffusion model to enhance photorealistic illumination, and introduces a Shading-Driven pipeline that separately optimizes albedo, shading, and specular residuals. The method achieves an 81.7% realism preference rate in user studies, significantly outperforming existing approaches.

## Background & Motivation

**Key Challenge**: Inserting 3D objects into video requires simultaneously satisfying two conflicting constraints:

**Temporal Consistency**: The inserted object must not flicker or abruptly change between frames; motion, viewpoint changes, and occlusions must all be handled correctly.

**Photorealistic Illumination**: The inserted object must blend naturally with the scene's lighting conditions, including shadows, highlights, and reflections.

**Limitations of Prior Work**

- **2D diffusion model approaches** (AnyV2V, ReVideo, ConsistI2V): These propagate edits made on the first frame using methods such as AnyDoor and can produce high visual quality, but 2D references lack the information necessary to synthesize consistent novel views, resulting in flickering and identity inconsistencies when the object moves or the viewpoint changes.
- **Traditional 3D rendering approaches**: AR pipelines naturally maintain temporal consistency but struggle to accurately model complex environmental lighting interactions, yielding unrealistic renderings that lack shadows, exhibit unnatural highlights, and fail to blend with the background.

**Key Insight**: The bracelet is an ideal research subject — it involves complex dynamic highlights (metallic/gemstone reflections), rapid and varied wrist motion, frequent occlusion changes (e.g., fingers occluding the bracelet), and places extremely high demands on both temporal consistency and illumination accuracy.

## Method

### Overall Architecture

The pipeline consists of three sequentially connected modules:

1. **Motion & Occlusion-Aware Calculation**
2. **Realism Enhancement**
3. **Temporal Smoothing**

### Module 1: Motion & Occlusion-Aware Insertion

This module follows a conventional AR pipeline:

- **Interactive GUI**: Users place the bracelet, adjust its pose (position/orientation/scale), and select tracking anchor points via a lightweight GUI.
- **2D Keypoint Tracking**: CoTracker tracks 2D keypoints on the skin to estimate cross-frame 3D pose changes.
- **Occlusion Handling**: Per-frame occlusion maps are generated by comparing the bracelet depth against scene depth estimated by UniDepth.
- **Initial Preview**: The 3DGS-rendered bracelet is composited onto the video frames.

### Module 2: Shading-Driven Realism Enhancement

The core contribution — rather than enhancing directly in RGB space, the pipeline first operates in the shading domain:

**Image Decomposition**: The linear RGB image is decomposed into three intrinsic components:

$$\tilde{\mathbf{I}}_t = \mathbf{A}_t \cdot \mathbf{S}_t + \mathbf{R}_t$$

where $\mathbf{A}_t$ is the albedo (reflectance), $\mathbf{S}_t$ is the shading (illumination effect), and $\mathbf{R}_t$ is the residual (e.g., specular highlights).

**Region Segmentation**: The scene is divided into three regions — the bracelet region $\mathbf{M}$, the background region $\mathbf{M}_{bg}$ (outside the expanded bounding box), and the surrounding region $\mathbf{M}_{surr}$ (the shadow-casting area near the bracelet). The enhancement model does not modify the background shading.

**Two-Stage Color Pipeline**:

1. **Bracelet Relighting Network $f_{br}$**: Takes bracelet shading, background shading, and a normal map as input, and outputs the relighted bracelet shading.
2. **Shadow Generation Network $f_{sh}$**: Takes the relighted shading, background, and surrounding shading as input, and generates complete shading with natural cast shadows.
3. **sRGB Enhancement Network $f_{sRGB}$**: Refines fine details in sRGB space.

**Network Architecture**: Based on fine-tuned Stable Diffusion, adopting a single-step diffusion formulation (zero latent → clean output) to maintain high fidelity to the input. The enhancement decoder injects multi-scale features extracted by an additional encoder $\mathcal{E}_f$ into the VAE decoder, preventing decoding from introducing bias.

**Training Data Generation**: Approximately 13,000 images of bracelets being worn are collected, and training pairs are constructed via data augmentation:
- Bracelet relighting: synthetic multi-light shading maps + grayscale blending + brightness inversion.
- Shadow generation: Gaussian blurring + random Gaussian blob insertion.

### Module 3: Temporal Smoothing

**Bracelet Smoothing**: The color attributes of 3DGS are optimized frame by frame using a sliding window with Gaussian weights to ensure smooth transitions between adjacent frames:

$$\mathcal{G}_t^* = \arg\min_{\mathcal{G}} \sum_{k=t-W/2}^{t+W/2} w(k-t) \cdot \|\mathcal{R}(\mathbf{K}, \mathbf{P}_k, \mathcal{G}) - \mathbf{I}_t^{\text{refined}}\|^2$$

Only color attributes are optimized to preserve geometric structure and identity.

**Shadow Smoothing**: Keyframes are uniformly sampled and EbSynth is used to interpolate intermediate frames. The bracelet mask is then used to blend the two results.

## Key Experimental Results

### Main Results: User Study

| Method | Realism↑ | Consistency↑ | Fidelity↑ |
|--------|----------|--------------|-----------|
| AnyV2V | 8.7% | 8.7% | 6.7% |
| ReVideo | 4.0% | 3.6% | 4.4% |
| ConsistI2V | 5.6% | 3.6% | 4.8% |
| **Ours** | **81.7%** | **84.1%** | **84.1%** |

Evaluated by 36 participants; the proposed method receives overwhelming preference across all three dimensions (81–84%).

### Quantitative Comparison

| Method | DeQA Score↑ | Temporal Consistency↑ | CLIP Score↑ |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|-------------|
| AnyV2V | 3.41 | 0.971 | 0.659 |
| ReVideo | 3.17 | 0.977 | 0.661 |
| ConsistI2V | 2.44 | 0.976 | 0.638 |
| **Ours** | **3.66** | **0.984** | **0.662** |

The proposed method achieves the best scores on all three metrics; temporal consistency of 0.984 is particularly notable, benefiting from the 3DGS intermediate representation.

### Ablation Study

**Shading-Driven Enhancement**: Compared to a baseline that directly predicts enhancement in sRGB space, the Shading-Driven pipeline produces richer bracelet detail and more natural lighting interactions; the baseline results appear flat and poorly integrated.

**Temporal Smoothing**:
- No smoothing: noticeable flickering and motion inconsistency.
- EbSynth only: smoother transitions but loss of detail.
- **3DGS smoothing** (proposed): achieves both smooth transitions and detail preservation.

## Highlights & Insights

1. **First successful 3D+2D hybrid paradigm**: 3DGS handles geometric consistency while 2D diffusion handles photorealistic illumination — the two complement each other's strengths and avoid each other's weaknesses.
2. **Elegant design of Shading-Driven decomposition**: Operating in shading space avoids unintended modification of color/texture information; treating shadows as a separate module increases flexibility.
3. **Justified choice of single-step diffusion**: Multi-step diffusion introduces stochasticity that degrades fidelity; single-step diffusion leverages SD priors while guaranteeing deterministic output.
4. **Engineering completeness of the interactive GUI**: Users can directly place and adjust the bracelet and select tracking anchor points, substantially lowering the barrier to practical use.

## Limitations & Future Work

- Limited ability to handle low-quality background video (motion blur, severe artifacts).
- Validated only on the bracelet scenario; generalization to arbitrary object insertion has not been demonstrated.
- Relies on the 2D tracking quality of CoTracker, which may fail under rapid hand motion.
- The training dataset (13K images) is relatively small, potentially limiting generalization to extreme lighting conditions.

## Related Work & Insights

- **AnyDoor**: Zero-shot object-level image customization; used in this paper to edit the first frame as input to the baseline.
- **3D Gaussian Splatting**: Employed as an efficient 3D representation for maintaining cross-view consistency.
- **Image Harmonization (Intrinsic Harmonization)**: The idea of harmonization in the shading domain inspired this work, though existing methods cannot handle cast shadows.
- **Insight**: The approach of using 3DGS as an intermediate representation for temporal smoothing is highly elegant and can be generalized to any scenario requiring consistent object appearance across video frames.
