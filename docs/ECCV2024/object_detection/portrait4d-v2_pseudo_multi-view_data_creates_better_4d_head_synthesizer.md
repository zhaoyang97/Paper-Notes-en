# Portrait4D-v2: Pseudo Multi-View Data Creates Better 4D Head Synthesizer

**Conference**: ECCV 2024  
**arXiv**: [2403.13570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13570)  
**Code**: None (Project page: [https://yudeng.github.io/Portrait4D-v2/](https://yudeng.github.io/Portrait4D-v2/))  
**Area**: Object Detection  
**Keywords**: 4D head synthesis, one-shot driving, pseudo multi-view, NeRF, face reenactment

## TL;DR

A new learning paradigm utilizing **pseudo multi-view videos** to train a feed-forward, one-shot 4D head synthesizer is proposed. It first learns a 3D head synthesizer from synthetic data to convert monocular videos into multi-view videos, and then trains the 4D synthesizer through **cross-view self-reenactment** using these pseudo multi-view videos. This avoids over-reliance on 3DMMs and significantly outperforms prior methods in reconstruction fidelity, geometric consistency, and motion control accuracy.

## Background & Motivation

One-shot head avatar synthesis is a highly active research direction in recent years, aiming to generate realistic portrait videos given a source image and a driving motion sequence. The technological paradigm has shifted from 2D generative models (generating 2D frames directly) to animatable 3D (i.e., 4D) synthesizers, which maintain better geometric consistency and support free-viewpoint rendering.

Key difficulties faced by 3D methods:
- **3D data scarcity**: The lack of large-scale multi-view video data makes learning 3D reconstruction from monocular videos highly ill-posed.
- **3DMM reliance**: Existing methods rely on 3D Morphable Models (3DMMs) to assist geometric and motion learning, but 3DMMs suffer from limited reconstruction accuracy and insufficient expressiveness.
- **Synthetic-real domain gap**: Portrait4D generates synthetic data through 4D GANs, but the facial expressions remain constrained by the 3DMM representation space.

The authors' key insight: If a **static 3D head synthesizer** (which only requires synthetic static multi-view images) is learned first, it can generalize to real images and convert each frame of a monocular video into multiple views. This allows the 4D synthesizer to be trained on pseudo multi-view videos, simultaneously obtaining: (1) the high scalability and rich expressions of monocular videos; (2) the geometric learning capability of multi-view data; (3) a motion representation that surpasses 3DMMs.

## Method

### Overall Architecture

The method is divided into two stages:

**First Stage**: Disable motion-related layers and train a static 3D head synthesizer $\Psi_{3d}$ on synthetic data for novel view synthesis.

**Second Stage**: Activate motion-related components and learn the full 4D synthesizer $\Psi$ through cross-view self-reenactment using pseudo multi-view versions of real videos.

### Key Designs

1. **ViT-based Triplane Reconstructor**: Adhering to the backbone architecture of Portrait4D, two CNN encoders extract global and detailed appearance features, respectively. The global features are fed into ViT blocks for pose regularization, and then concatenated with detailed features to be sent into a ViT decoder to generate the triplane $T$. Motion information is injected via motion-aware cross-attention layers: the cross-attention of the first few blocks receives the motion embedding $v_s$ of the source image for neutralization, while the subsequent blocks receive the motion embedding $v_d$ of the driving image for reenactment. Motion embeddings are extracted via a pretrained 2D motion encoder $E_{mot}$.

2. **3D Synthesizer and Multi-view Video Generation**: By disabling the cross-attention layers in the full reconstructor, the static 3D synthesizer $\Psi_{3d}$ is obtained. Synthetic multi-view data generated by GenHead (a 4D GAN of Portrait4D) is used to train $\Psi_{3d}$, with the learning objective of reconstructing the image of the same 3D head at any target view from the input image. The training loss contains 7 terms:
    $\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_1 + \mathcal{L}_{LPIPS} + \mathcal{L}_{id} + \mathcal{L}_{adv} + \mathcal{L}_{depth} + \mathcal{L}_{opa} + \mathcal{L}_T$
   
   Key Findings: Although $\Psi_{3d}$ is trained only on static images, it maintains geometric consistency across video frames and preserves the detailed shape and expressions of real frames better than Portrait4D. This is because static reconstruction utilizes fewer 3DMM inductive biases.

3. **Cross-View Self-Reenactment Learning**: The core training strategy. In each iteration:

    - Sample a source frame $\hat{I}_s$ from a real video.
    - Sample two different views of the same motion frame, $I_d(\theta_p)$ and $I_d(\theta_q)$, from the pseudo multi-view driving video.
    - Use $\hat{I}_s$ as the appearance input and extract the motion embedding from $I_d(\theta_p)$, enabling $\Psi$ to generate the reenactment result from the viewpoint of $\theta_q$.
    - Training loss: $$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_1 + \mathcal{L}_{LPIPS} + \mathcal{L}_{id} + \mathcal{L}_{adv}$$
   
   **Replacement Strategy**: Since $\Psi_{3d}$ is imperfect, $I_d(\theta_p)$ is replaced with the real driving frame $\hat{I}_d$ with a 10% probability, and $I_d(\theta_q)$ is replaced with $\hat{I}_d$ with an 80% probability. This encourages the model to primarily learn to reconstruct the real frames, while the pseudo multi-view frames act more as geometric regularization. When using the real frame as the ground truth (GT), all 4 loss terms are applied; otherwise, only the LPIPS loss is utilized.

   **Effects of Cross-View Learning**: (1) Distills the geometric and appearance priors of the 3D synthesizer into the 4D synthesizer; (2) Enables the usage of non-3D-aware motion embeddings—by extracting motion embeddings from the driving frame at an arbitrary viewpoint, pose information is squeezed out, achieving disentanglement of expression and pose.

### Loss & Training

- 3D stage: Adam optimizer, lr=1e-4, batch=32, trained until seeing 10M images.
- 4D stage: Trained on 10K video clips from VFHQ, trained until seeing 6M images. Lr=2.5e-4 for motion-related layers, lr=1e-4 for other components.
- The 4D model is initialized with the weights of the 3D model (except for the motion-related components which are randomly initialized).

## Key Experimental Results

**Self-Reenactment and Cross-Identity Reenactment (VFHQ, 512² resolution)**

Key ablation findings:
- 3D initialization is critical (Config A vs K), providing geometric priors.
- Cross-view driving frames significantly improve geometric consistency (Cons.) (Config B vs K).
- The Emot motion encoder outperforms 3DMMs (Config F vs K), delivering richer expressions.
- The volume of data has a significant impact: performance continuously improves from 1K to 10K videos.

### Key Findings

- Inference speed is 10 FPS (reaches 15 FPS after caching source features).
- Works even on artistic portrait styles.
- Although the 3D synthesizer is trained on static images, it maintains good geometric consistency across video frames.
- Cross-view learning effectively distills 3D priors into the 4D synthesizer while preserving detailed motion control.

## Highlights & Insights

- **Learning Paradigm Innovation**: Instead of directly generating 4D data or relying on 3DMMs, it elegantly uses a 3D synthesizer to "uplift" monocular videos to multi-view, and then vice versa to learn the 4D synthesizer. The idea is elegant and practical.
- **Exquisite Disentanglement Strategy**: Pose and expression are naturally disentangled by extracting motion embeddings from cross-view driving frames.
- **Replacement Probability Design**: The 80%/10% replacement strategy achieves a great balance between real data fidelity and geometric regularization.
- **Excellent Result Quality**: The user study shows an 80.7% quality preference rate, far exceeding all competing methods.

## Limitations & Future Work

- Inference speed (10-15 FPS) still falls short of real-time applications.
- The 3D synthesizer $\Psi_{3d}$ is learned on FFHQ via GenHead, meaning domain discrepancies still exist between synthetic and real data.
- Body parts below the neck are not modeled.
- Robustness to extreme lighting and occlusions has not been fully evaluated.
- The core strength of the method stems from the learning paradigm rather than the network architecture; future work can integrate more advanced backbones to further step up performance.

## Related Work & Insights

- Belongs to the same technical line as Portrait4D, but avoids the 3DMM limitations of 4D GANs through pseudo multi-view data.
- The concept can be generalized to other monocular reconstruction problems requiring 3D priors (such as full-body avatars, hand reconstruction, etc.).
- The 3D-to-4D knowledge distillation paradigm yields valuable insights for acquiring training data for generative models.
- Topology-agnostic methods like DiffusionNet (e.g., ScanTalk from the same batch) represent breakthroughs in a different direction.
