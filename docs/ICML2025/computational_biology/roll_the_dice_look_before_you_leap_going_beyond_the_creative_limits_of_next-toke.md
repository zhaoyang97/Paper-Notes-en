# Roll the Dice & Look Before You Leap: Going Beyond the Creative Limits of Next-Token Prediction

**Conference**: ICML2025 Oral  
**arXiv**: [2504.15266](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15266)  
**Code**: [chenwu98/algorithmic-creativity](https://github.com/chenwu98/algorithmic-creativity)  
**Area**: LLM/NLP  
**Keywords**: next-token prediction, multi-token prediction, creativity, teacherless training, diffusion, seed-conditioning, algorithmic creativity

## TL;DR

This paper designs a suite of minimal algorithmic tasks to quantify the "creative limits" of language models, demonstrating that next-token prediction (NTP) learning is short-sighted in open-ended tasks requiring "leaps of thought," whereas multi-token methods (such as teacherless training and discrete diffusion models) and input-layer noise injection (seed-conditioning) can significantly improve generative diversity and originality.

## Background & Motivation

Current language models show limited performance in open-ended creative tasks (such as designing math problems, generating research ideas, or formulating puns). The core characteristic of these tasks is the requirement of an **implicit, stochastic "leap of thought"**—meaning that before generating output, the model must pre-plan multiple highly correlated, random decisions.

Existing evaluation methods face two major difficulties: (1) creativity assessment of real-world tasks is highly subjective; (2) model training data covers the entire internet, making originality difficult to define. Consequently, the authors turn to **controllable algorithmic tasks**, designing loose abstractions to objectively quantify the creativity of models.

Drawing from cognitive science literature (Boden, 2003), the authors identify two fundamental modes of creativity:

- **Combinational Creativity**: Discovering novel, multi-hop connections in a known graph of relationships (e.g., puns require searching a semantic graph to find a "bridge word" connecting two unrelated concepts).
- **Exploratory Creativity**: Constructing entirely new patterns under rule constraints (e.g., designing solvable math problems requires constructing new structures that satisfy logical constraints).

## Method

### Algorithmic Creativity Metric

Given a set of samples $T$ generated by the model, the algorithmic creativity metric is defined as:

$$\hat{\text{cr}}_N(T) = \frac{\text{uniq}(\{s \in T \mid \neg \text{mem}_S(s) \wedge \text{coh}(s)\})}{|T|}$$

Where $\text{coh}(s)$ assesses coherence, $\text{mem}_S(s)$ determines whether a sample is memorized from the training set, and $\text{uniq}(\cdot)$ computes the number of unique samples. This metric simultaneously measures **coherence + originality + diversity**.

### Four Algorithmic Tasks

| Task | Creativity Type | Core Requirement | Nature of Leap of Thought |
|------|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Sibling Discovery | Combinational | Generate novel sibling-parent triplets $(\gamma, \gamma', \Gamma)$ in a bipartite graph | Requires planning the parent node (the punchline of a pun) before generating sibling nodes |
| Triangle Discovery | Combinational | Generate novel triangles $(v_1, v_2, v_3)$ in a knowledge graph | Requires coordinating three edges simultaneously, a more complex high-order planning |
| Circle Construction | Exploratory | Generate an adjacency list that can be reordered into a cycle graph | Requires constructing a novel permutation $\pi$ as an implicit plan |
| Line Construction | Exploratory | Generate an adjacency list that can be reordered into a line graph | Similar to cycle construction but with a different termination condition |

### Why NTP is Limited in These Tasks

Core argument: The autoregressive decomposition of NTP $p(s_i | s_{<i}, z)$ is misled by **Clever Hans cheating**. Taking Sibling Discovery as an example:

1. Ideal generation process: first plan the parent node $z := \Gamma$, and then independently sample sibling nodes according to $p(\gamma | z)$, which only requires $O(m \cdot n)$ data.
2. Actual NTP learning: When predicting $\Gamma$, the model has already seen $(\gamma, \gamma')$ and directly exploits cues in the input (the Clever Hans cheat), resulting in a lack of gradient signals to learn the implicit planning $z$.
3. Consequence: The model learns the second sibling node via $p(\gamma' | \gamma)$, requiring $O(m \cdot n^2)$ data, which is $n$ times less efficient.

### Multi-token Methods

- **Teacherless Training**: Masking the true sequence $s$ in the input with dummy tokens; the model predicts tokens at all positions simultaneously based solely on the prompt $p$, preventing Clever Hans cheating.
- **Discrete Diffusion Models (SEDD)**: Starting from a fully masked/corrupted sequence and iteratively denoising to restore all tokens, naturally possessing a global field of view.

### Seed-Conditioning

Traditional diversity originates from temperature sampling at the output layer, but this can lead to "cognitive overload"—where the model must process multiple conceptual threads simultaneously to calculate the marginalized token distribution.

The seed-conditioning scheme: During training, associate each sample with a random, meaningless prefix string (the seed); during inference, generate new samples using a new seed. Intuitively, fixing a random seed allows the model to focus on a single line of thought instead of maintaining multiple running ideas.

## Key Experimental Results

### Main Results: Effect of Multi-token Training on Gemma v1 (2B)

| Task | NTP Creativity | Multi-token Creativity | Gain | NTP Memorization Rate | Multi-token Memorization Rate |
|------|-----------|---------------|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Sibling Discovery | ~0.05 | ~0.25 | **~5×** | High | Significantly Reduced |
| Triangle Discovery | ~0.04 | ~0.20 | **~5×** | High | Significantly Reduced |
| Circle Construction | Low | Medium | Improved | High | Reduced |
| Line Construction | Low | Medium | Improved | High | Reduced |

### Small Model Comparison: GPT-2 (86M) vs SEDD (90M)

The diffusion model SEDD significantly outperforms the NTP-trained GPT-2 in 3 out of 4 tasks (performing slightly worse on Sibling Discovery). Teacherless training offers limited gains for small models (due to optimization difficulties), but the creativity of top-K samples is still improved.

### Effects of Seed-Conditioning

- Even using **greedy decoding** (temperature=0), seed-conditioning is capable of producing non-trivial creativity scores.
- Increasing seed length consistently improves creativity.
- The creativity of seed-conditioning is **comparable to or even better** than temperature sampling (especially in exploratory creativity tasks).
- Under a fixed temperature, adding a seed prefix is always superior to having no prefix.

### Real-world Task Validation: Summary Generation

On XSUM and CNN/DailyMail, large language models trained with teacherless training achieved higher diversity (Self-BLEU) under identical quality constraints (Rouge), while consistently improving summarization quality.

## Highlights & Insights

1. **Minimal, Controllable Testbed**: Isolates the core computational demands of creativity through minimalist algorithmic tasks, bypassing the subjectivity and lack of control associated with real-world task evaluation.
2. **New Argument on NTP Short-Sightedness**: Unlike the reasoning correctness gap presented in B&N'24, this paper demonstrates a **diversity gap** that appears even in simple tasks requiring only a 2-token lookahead.
3. **Permutation Invariance**: Triangle Discovery, Circle/Line Construction are permutation invariant—no amount of token reordering is friendly to NTP, posing a challenge to permutation-based improvements (such as XLNet-style methods).
4. **Surprising Discovery of Seed-Conditioning**: Models can transform arbitrary, meaningless random prefixes into meaningful randomness, yielding diverse outputs even with deterministic decoding.
5. **Memorization Analysis**: The lack of creativity in NTP primarily stems from the **over-memorization** of training data rather than incoherent generation; multi-token methods drastically reduce memorization.

## Limitations & Future Work

1. **Limitations of Minimal Tasks**: Algorithmic tasks are extreme simplifications of real-world creativity; success here does not guarantee success in complex tasks (though failure here guarantees failure in complex tasks as well).
2. **Optimization Hardship of Teacherless Training**: Limited effectiveness on small models, consistent with findings in Gloeckle et al. (2024).
3. **Seed-Conditioning Requires Dedicated Training**: It is not as plug-and-play as temperature sampling and offers no gain on diffusion models.
4. **Under-Exploratory**: Model scaling effects, pre-training impact, and the comparison of in-context learning vs. fine-tuning have not been thoroughly analyzed.
5. **Narrow Definition of Creativity**: Only covers two categories from Boden’s taxonomy, omitting Transformative Creativity and "Big-C" creativity.
6. **In-Distribution Creativity**: The originality measured in this paper is novelty within the training distribution, without addressing out-of-distribution generalization.

## Related Work & Insights

- **Bachmann & Nagarajan (2024)**: The path-star task demonstrates NTP’s limitations in reasoning correctness; this paper extends the discussion to the dimensions of creativity and diversity.
- **Khona et al. (2024)**: Diversity-accuracy tradeoff in path-connectivity tasks; this paper demonstrates that multi-token methods can resolve/improve this tradeoff.
- **Allen-Zhu & Li (2023)**: NTP can learn complex CFGs with large scales of data; the negative results in this paper do not contradict this (it exhibits sub-optimality under small data regimes).
- **Lou et al. (2023) SEDD**: Discrete diffusion models, which are adopted as a multi-token baseline in this paper.
- **DeSalvo et al. (2024)**: Inducing diversity via variable soft-prompts, which is conceptually similar to the seed-conditioning approach.

## Rating

- Novelty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Systematically quantifies the creative limits of NTP from the perspective of algorithmic tasks; the angle is unique and highly inspiring.
- Experimental Thoroughness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Four types of tasks + two model scales + ablation studies + real-world task validation, which is relatively comprehensive, though model scaling is limited.
- Writing Quality: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Clear motivation, rigorous arguments, and naturally connected with cognitive science literature.
- Value: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Provides new theoretical backing for multi-token prediction and seed-conditioning, though the gap between minimal tasks and real-world applications remains to be validated.
