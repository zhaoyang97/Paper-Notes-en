# Banana100: Breaking NR-IQA Metrics by 100 Iterative Image Replications with Nano Banana Pro

**Conference**: CVPR 2026  
**arXiv**: [2604.03400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03400)  
**Code**: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/kenantang/Banana100](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kenantang/Banana100)  
**Area**: Image Generation / Image Quality Assessment  
**Keywords**: Image Quality Assessment, NR-IQA, Iterative Degradation, image editing, Multi-turn

## TL;DR
Banana100 systematically investigates quality degradation in multi-turn editing by having Nano Banana Pro iteratively replicate images 100 times, constructing a dataset of 28,000 degraded images. The study reveals a startling finding: 21 mainstream No-Reference Image Quality Assessment (NR-IQA) metrics fail to reliably detect iterative degradation—most metrics even assign higher scores to noisy images than to clean ones.

## Background & Motivation

**Background**: The rise of multimodal Agent systems has made iterative image editing a norm, where users repeatedly modify and refine generated images. Modern models (e.g., Nano Banana Pro) perform excellently in single-turn editing.

**Limitations of Prior Work—Iterative Degradation**:
   - Each edit introduces tiny, nearly imperceptible artifacts.
   - When the output is reused as input, these artifacts are **cumulatively amplified**.
   - After approximately 5-10 steps, visible static noise, color shifts (greenish tints), and speckles appear.
   - Beyond visual quality degradation, the model's **instruction-following capability also declines** (e.g., failing to "add an apple on the table").

**Root Cause—Evaluator Failure**:
   - Only 2 of 23 NR-IQA metrics consistently detect the degradation.
   - **Startling Case**: Simple image replication caused the BRISQUE score to drop from 34.1 to -9.8 (lower is better), despite severe noise visible to the human eye.
   - Generator failure + Evaluator failure = Low-quality synthetic data may **silently leak** into training sets.

**Potential Consequences**:
   - **Training side**: If evaluators cannot filter noisy data, future model training data will become increasingly "dirty," accelerating model collapse.
   - **Inference side**: Agent systems are known to be fragile in long-sequence operations; quality check failures further exacerbate fragmentation.

**Goal**: To build a systematic dataset, classify failure modes, and expose the systematic flaws of NR-IQA.

## Method

### Dataset Construction

1. **Initial Images**: 13 high-quality initial images (11 generated by Nano Banana Pro + 2 generated by SPICE) at minimum 2K resolution.
    - Covers diverse contents and textures: skyscrapers, food, moss, peacock feathers, sand dunes, etc.
    - Human faces were deliberately excluded (due to visual discomfort after deformation).

2. **Iterative Editing Prompts**:
    - **Main Setting** (Replication): "Produce an exact replica of the provided image, with no alterations."
    - Variant testing: Different phrasing, mirror flipping, desaturation + colorization, different hyperparameters (seed/temperature/resolution).
    - Object addition testing: Gradually adding apples on a table / 100 different fruits.

3. **Scale**: 100 steps per setting × 5 repetitions → Total 28,000 images, costing approximately $4,000.

4. **Multi-model Validation**: Nano Banana 2 Fast, FLUX.2 [dev], Qwen Image Edit (1,400 images each).

### Failure Mode Classification

1. **Sub-object Level Failures**:
    - **Simplification Preference**: The model simplifies 4 eye colors (red-orange-purple-blue) into only 2 (red-blue) during replication, capturing only the most salient colors in the reasoning summary.

2. **Object Level Failures**:
    - **Counting Failures**: Failing to add an apple or adding an extra row when requested.
    - **Replacement instead of Addition**: Replacing existing fruit with new ones instead of adding them.
    - **Persistent Background Degradation**: Newly added objects have better quality, but background noise continues to worsen.

3. **Image Level Failures**:
    - **Aspect Ratio Mismatch**: Replication almost always results in image cropping.
    - **Persistent Noise**: Noise cannot be eliminated even when explicitly requested.
    - **Failure to Utilize Clean Context**: The model fails to refer to earlier clean images even within multi-turn dialogues.
    - **Mirror/Rotation Failure**: Mirror success rates for animation-style images are much lower than for realistic ones; rotation success is low for both.

### NR-IQA Evaluation

- Tested 21 NR-IQA metrics (pyiqa library) + 2 Large VLM-based metrics.
- Evaluation logic: Comparing normalized score differences $\Delta_i$ between Step 1 and Step 5/10/20.
- **Success Criteria**: $\Delta_5, \Delta_{10}, \Delta_{20}$ should all be negative (correctly identifying degradation).
- **Results**: All 21 traditional metrics **failed**—none could consistently detect degradation across all images.

## Key Experimental Results

### Main Results: NR-IQA Metric Failure Statistics

| Metric Type | Representative | Can Detect Degradation | Notes |
|----------|------|-------------|------|
| Hand-crafted | BRISQUE, NIQE, PIQE | ❌ | Higher scores for noisy images in most cases |
| CNN-based | CNNIQA, DBCNN, HyperIQA | ❌ | Failure modes vary by image |
| CLIP-based | CLIPIQA, LIQE, QualiCLIP | ❌ | Confused by model-generated texture patterns |
| Transformer-based | MUSIQ, MANIQA, TReS | ❌ | Also fails to detect consistently |
| **Large VLM-based** | **VisualQuality-R1, RALI** | **✅** | 0 failure cases for both |

### Ablation Study: Noise Accumulation Across Models

| Model | Noise Type | Notes |
|------|---------|------|
| Nano Banana Pro | Static noise + Green tint | Most common |
| Nano Banana 2 Fast | Creases along object contours | No reasoning mode |
| FLUX.2 [dev] | Speckles | Open-source model |
| Qwen Image Edit | Texture simplification + Object repetition | Moves right side to left side |

### Key Findings
- **All tested image editing models** exhibit iterative degradation issues, with varying noise patterns.
- Nano Banana Pro's reasoning summary contains severe hallucinations—claiming success in denoising when it actually failed.
- The models' self-assessment (reasoning summary) only mentions noise for the first time at steps 20-37 on average, lagging significantly behind human perception (steps 5-10).
- Open-source models (FLUX.2, Qwen) are equally affected → Not an exclusive issue caused by watermarking.
- Only the latest metrics based on Large VLMs (VisualQuality-R1, RALI) successfully detected all degradations.

## Highlights & Insights
- **Reveals a systematic rather than anecdotal problem**: All mainstream editing models degrade + all mainstream NR-IQA fail = Ecosystem-level risk.
- **The combined effect of "Generator failure × Evaluator failure"** is dangerous: Low-quality data might flow into training sets without being filtered.
- The classification of failure modes (sub-object/object/image level) provides clear targets for future improvements.
- The success of only Large VLM-based metrics suggests a needed paradigm shift in the NR-IQA field.

## Limitations & Future Work
- The initial set included only 13 images, all AI-generated—conclusions might differ for real photos (which have compression artifacts).
- Mean Opinion Score (MOS) annotations were not included, so NR-IQA rankings could not be evaluated using PLCC/SRCC.
- Reproducibility of black-box API models is limited.
- While the problem is identified, no solution is proposed—how to fix the editing models or NR-IQA metrics remains future work.

## Related Work & Insights
- The multi-step subset of the Pico-Banana-400K dataset already showed obvious degradation (after steps 5-6), consistent with findings in this paper.
- REED-VAE attempts to reduce iterative editing degradation in diffusion models, but with limited effectiveness.
- A major warning for Agent evaluation frameworks—if the underlying evaluation metrics are unreliable, the Agent's autonomous decisions are also unreliable.
- An appeal to the NR-IQA community to focus on model-generated noise (vs. traditional artificial noise) as training data.

## Rating
- Novelty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ First to systematically reveal the dual problem of iterative editing degradation and NR-IQA failure.
- Experimental Thoroughness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 models × 23 metrics × multiple editing methods; extremely comprehensive coverage.
