# SA-LUT: Spatial Adaptive 4D Look-Up Table for Photorealistic Style Transfer

**Conference**: ICCV 2025
**arXiv**: [2506.13465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13465)
**Code**: [https://github.com/Ry3nG/SA-LUT](https://github.com/Ry3nG/SA-LUT)
**Area**: Image Generation / Style Transfer
**Keywords**: Photorealistic Style Transfer, 4D LUT, Spatial Adaptivity, Context Map, Real-Time Color Grading

## TL;DR

This paper proposes SA-LUT, which achieves spatially adaptive photorealistic style transfer via a style-guided 4D look-up table and a context map generated by content-style cross-attention. On the newly introduced PST50 benchmark, SA-LUT reduces LPIPS by 66.7% compared to 3D LUT methods while supporting real-time 4K video processing at 16 FPS.

## Background & Motivation

**Challenges in photorealistic style transfer (PST)**: PST requires transferring color characteristics while strictly preserving content structure, which is fundamentally different from artistic style transfer that tolerates geometric distortion. Video applications further demand real-time processing and temporal consistency.

**Trade-offs of existing methods**:
- **Generative methods** (e.g., WCT2): High fidelity but computationally expensive and prone to structural artifacts.
- **Global color transforms** (3D LUT): Efficient but lack spatial adaptivity — pixels with identical colors are mapped identically regardless of their semantic region (e.g., sky vs. ocean).
- **Neural LUT**: Style-aware but still constrained by the spatial invariance of 3D LUTs.

**Core innovation of SA-LUT**: A fourth (context) dimension is introduced into the LUT to enable spatial semantic awareness, while retaining the hardware-friendly efficiency of conventional LUTs.

## Method

### Overall Architecture

SA-LUT consists of two core components and operates in the LOG color space.

### Key Design 1: Style-Guided 4D LUT Generator

**Style encoding and weight generation**:

$$f_{\text{concat}} = \text{Concat}_{d=1}^{4}(\text{Pool}_{\max}(\text{Conv}(F_s^{(d)})))$$
$$\alpha = \text{Softmax}(\text{MLP}(f_{\text{concat}}))$$

- VGG is used to extract multi-scale style features $F_s^{(1)},\dots,F_s^{(4)}$ from the style image.
- After convolution, pooling, and concatenation, an MLP with Softmax produces a weight vector $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}^N$.

**LUT fusion**: $N$ learnable basis LUTs $\text{LUT}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 2 \times D \times D \times D}$ are maintained and combined via weighted summation to produce the style-specific 4D LUT:

$$LUT_{\text{fused}} = LUT_{\text{identity}} + \sum_{i=1}^{N} \alpha_i \cdot LUT_i$$

- Dimensions $3 \times 2 \times D \times D \times D$: 3 RGB channels × 2 context bins × $D^3$ color grid.
- An identity LUT serves as a residual connection, ensuring the input is preserved when $\alpha \to 0$.
- $D=17$, $N=64$ basis LUTs.

### Key Design 2: Context Generator

A content-specific context map $\Gamma \in [0,1]^{H \times W}$ is generated as follows:

$$\text{Attn}(Q, K) = \text{Softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d}}\right)$$

- Content features serve as Query; style features provide Key and Value.
- Cross-attention establishes region-level correspondences between content and style.
- The output is processed through convolution and upsampling to yield a single-channel context map.

### Quadrilinear Interpolation

$$I_p^{RGB} = \text{Quad}(LUT_{\text{fused}}, [\Gamma, I_c^{LOG}])$$

The context map $\Gamma$ is concatenated with the content image to form a 4-channel input, which is then used for quadrilinear interpolation in the 4D LUT. Each pixel is blended between two 3D LUT "slices" according to its context value, enabling a continuous and spatially adaptive transformation.

### Loss & Training

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \lambda_1\mathcal{L}_{\text{lpips}} + \lambda_2\mathcal{L}_{\text{TV}} + \lambda_3\mathcal{L}_{\text{MN}} + \lambda_4\mathcal{L}_{\text{adv}}$$

A dual-stream data strategy is adopted:
- **Synthetic stream**: Professional 3D LUTs are applied to LOG images to generate ground-truth pairs, supervised with LPIPS loss.
- **Real stream**: Two crops are extracted from the same photograph; one serves as the style reference, and the other is converted to LOG input via Style2Log, trained with adversarial loss.

## Key Experimental Results

### Main Results (PST50 Paired)

| Method | LPIPS↓ | PSNR↑ | SSIM↑ | H-Corr↑ | Inference Time (s) |
|--------|--------|-------|-------|---------|-------------------|
| AdaIN | 0.53 | 18.21 | 0.62 | 0.39 | 0.0499 |
| NLUT | 0.36 | 20.59 | 0.80 | 0.33 | 16.11+0.0003 |
| WCT2 | 0.27 | 19.86 | 0.81 | 0.31 | 0.6600 |
| Neural Preset | 0.19 | 23.03 | 0.89 | 0.44 | N/A |
| **SA-LUT** | **0.12** | **25.29** | **0.92** | **0.51** | 0.21+0.01 |

### Ablation Study

| Configuration | LPIPS↓ | H-Corr↑ |
|---------------|--------|---------|
| w/o Context Generator (3D LUT) | 0.14 | 0.38 |
| w/o Cross-Attention | 0.13 | 0.46 |
| **Full SA-LUT** | **0.12** | **0.51** |

### Key Findings

- LPIPS is reduced by 66.7% relative to NLUT (0.12 vs. 0.36), with a 4.7 dB gain in PSNR.
- LUT generation is 75× faster than NLUT (0.21s vs. 16.11s).
- In user studies, SA-LUT achieves a preference rate of 48.79% (vs. Neural Preset 33.6%, NLUT 17.6%).
- Processing speed exceeds 16 FPS on 4K video, meeting professional real-time color grading requirements.
- The context map effectively distinguishes between bright and dark regions within the same semantic category (e.g., highlights and shadows on ocean surfaces).

## Highlights & Insights

1. **PST50 Benchmark**: The first photorealistic style transfer evaluation benchmark with ground truth, comprising 100 paired/unpaired image pairs.
2. **Efficiency without sacrificing quality**: Once generated, the LUT can be reused across multiple images and video frames.
3. **Two context bins suffice**: Continuous context maps combined with quadrilinear interpolation create a dense continuum of transformations.

## Limitations & Future Work

- Only 2 context bins limit adaptivity in highly complex scenes.
- Reliance on VGG for style feature extraction may reduce robustness on non-natural images.
- The PST50 dataset is relatively small (100 pairs), limiting statistical significance.

## Related Work & Insights

- **LUT-based methods**: NLUT, Image-Adaptive 3D LUT
- **Generative PST**: WCT2, PhotoWCT, DPST
- **Preset-based methods**: Deep Preset, Neural Preset, Modulated Flow
