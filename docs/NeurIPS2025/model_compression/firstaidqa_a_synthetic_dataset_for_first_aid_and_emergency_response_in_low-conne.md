# FirstAidQA: A Synthetic Dataset for First Aid and Emergency Response in Low-Connectivity Settings

**Conference**: NeurIPS 2025 (Workshop: Muslims in ML)  
**arXiv**: [2511.01289](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01289)  
**Code**: [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/i-am-mushfiq/FirstAidQA)  
**Area**: Dataset Construction / Medical NLP  
**Keywords**: Synthetic dataset, first aid QA, offline AI, low-resource deployment, small language models, instruction tuning

## TL;DR
This paper introduces FirstAidQA, a dataset of 5,500 synthetic first aid question-answer pairs generated by ChatGPT-4o-mini from certified first aid textbooks and validated by human experts, designed to support fine-tuning of first aid AI systems in low-connectivity or offline environments.

## Background & Motivation

**Background**: The deployment of LLMs in safety-critical, real-time applications such as first aid and emergency response remains extremely limited. A primary barrier is the lack of high-quality, domain-specific QA datasets for the first aid domain.

**Deployment Scenario Requirements**: Disaster zones, rural clinics, remote areas, and economically disadvantaged regions typically lack high-speed internet and modern computing infrastructure, necessitating lightweight models capable of offline operation.

**Limitations of Prior Work**: Existing datasets such as BioASQ, MedQA, and PubMedQA primarily target clinical diagnosis and biomedical literature, and do not cover **step-by-step procedural knowledge for first aid actions performed by non-professionals**.

**Goal**: To create the first synthetic QA dataset specifically targeting first aid and emergency response, supporting instruction tuning and fine-tuning of LLMs and SLMs.

## Method

### Data Source Selection
- Based on the **Vital First Aid Book (2019)**, a certified first aid textbook
- Content covers: DRSABCD protocol, CPR, traffic accident management, casualty transport, first aid equipment use, patient assessment, and medical conditions including asthma, bleeding, burns, fractures, head trauma, and temperature-related emergencies
- Compliant with international standards (American Red Cross, ILCOR)

### Taxonomy

| Category | Subcategories / Description |
|------|-----------|
| General First Aid Procedures | Preparation, prioritization, DRSABCD protocol |
| CPR | Adult/child/infant standard methods; special cases including drowning, choking, and overdose |
| Traffic Accidents | Scene safety, casualty assessment and rescue, multi-casualty management |
| Casualty Transport | Transport methods, spinal protection, single-person vs. team scenarios |
| First Aid Equipment & Techniques | First aid kits, dressings, slings, splints, improvised tools |
| Home & Community Safety | Home preparedness and community-level emergency response |
| Patient Examination & Monitoring | Vital signs, injury severity, temperature assessment |
| Specific Medical Conditions | Respiratory, bleeding, burns, fractures, head and facial trauma, cardiovascular, neurological, bites, poisoning, etc. |
| Cervical Spine Injuries | Assessment, immobilization, safe handling |

### Synthetic Data Generation Pipeline

1. **Text Chunking**: The textbook is segmented into context-preserving chunks by topic.
2. **Prompt Design**:
    - Role assignment: expert in synthetic dataset creation for first aid and medical emergencies
    - Input: topic-specific textbook text segments
    - Requirements: medically accurate, step-by-step, actionable QA pairs
    - Multi-perspective coverage: bystander, trained rescuer, lone rescuer, etc.
    - Multi-scenario coverage: accidents, extreme weather, confined spaces, etc.
    - Output format: JSON
3. **Iterative Expansion**: Starting with 20 pairs per topic, iterative prompting ("generate 20 more non-overlapping pairs") until approximately 100 pairs per topic are obtained.
4. **Total Volume**: 5,500 QA pairs

### Quality Assurance

1. **Filtering**: Text chunks capable of generating practically applicable QA pairs are selected.
2. **Safety Check**: Prompts require generation strictly based on the provided text segments to mitigate hallucination.
3. **Expert Human Validation**: Three medical professionals evaluated 200 randomly sampled pairs.

Evaluation dimensions and scores:

| Evaluation Dimension | Mean Score (1–5) |
|----------|:------------:|
| Clarity | 4.2 |
| Relevance | 4.7 |
| Specificity & Completeness | 4.0 |
| Safety & Accuracy | 3.7 |

### Examples of Safety Issues
The paper's appendix explicitly lists potentially unsafe responses identified during validation:
- Bee sting allergy management: incorrectly recommends compression immobilization
- CPR choking response: recommends blind finger sweep, a technique no longer endorsed
- Marine animal stings: incorrectly conflates vinegar treatment (appropriate for jellyfish) with stingray injuries (which require hot water immersion)
- Tick removal: recommends freezing spray, which is discouraged due to increased risk of toxin release

## Key Experimental Results

### Dataset Scale
- Total: 5,500 QA pairs
- Coverage: 9 major categories, 20+ subcategories
- Generation model: ChatGPT-4o-mini
- Reference source: single certified textbook
- Format: JSON, directly usable in ML pipelines

### Human Validation Statistics
- Validated samples: 200 (randomly sampled)
- Evaluators: 3 medical professionals
- Safety & Accuracy received the lowest score (3.7/5), indicating that synthetic data in safety-critical domains still warrants caution

### Comparison with Related Benchmarks

| Dataset | Scale | Domain | Source |
|--------|------|------|------|
| BioASQ | Expert-annotated | Biomedical | Literature |
| MedQuAD | 47K | Consumer health | NIH websites |
| MedMCQA | Large-scale | Medical exams | Exam questions |
| COVID-QA | ~2K | COVID-19 | Scientific literature |
| **FirstAidQA** | **5.5K** | **First aid** | **Certified textbook + LLM synthesis** |

## Highlights & Insights
- **Gap Filling**: This is the first QA dataset dedicated to the first aid domain; prior work is limited to FAQ-based chatbots or scattered evaluations of Siri, Alexa, and ChatGPT.
- **Deployment-Oriented**: Explicitly targets low-connectivity and offline scenarios, suited for fine-tuning SLMs (e.g., TinyLlama-1.1B with LoRA).
- **Transparent Quality Reporting**: Unsafe sample cases are candidly disclosed, helping downstream users identify and mitigate risks.
- **Open Access**: The dataset is publicly released on HuggingFace.

## Limitations & Future Work
- **Safety Concerns**: A Safety & Accuracy score of only 3.7/5 poses elevated risk in safety-critical applications; further data cleaning is needed.
- **Single-Source Bias**: Reliance on a single first aid textbook may introduce regional or cultural bias (e.g., variation in first aid standards across countries).
- **No Model Training Experiments**: No models are actually trained or fine-tuned to validate dataset utility.
- **No Comparative Evaluation**: The generated QA pairs are not benchmarked against existing FAQ systems or outputs from large LLMs.
- **Limited Scale**: 5,500 pairs may be insufficient for instruction tuning.
- **English Only**: No coverage of other languages.
- **Workshop Paper**: Depth and completeness are inherently limited.
- **Cannot Replace Professional Medical Help**: Explicitly stated in the paper.

## Related Work & Insights
- **vs. Self-Instruct**: Borrows prompt engineering methodology from Self-Instruct but restricts generation to certified sources within the first aid domain.
- **vs. EHR-DS-QA**: Generates 156K QA pairs from discharge summaries for clinical retrieval; FirstAidQA targets non-professional first aid.
- **vs. TinyLlama QLoRA**: Cahlen's work provides a LoRA adapter for offline practical skills QA; FirstAidQA offers more systematically structured training data.
- **vs. Dr.FirstAider**: A rule-driven FAQ chatbot; FirstAidQA supports end-to-end neural model training.

## Rating
- **Novelty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ First QA dataset dedicated to first aid, though the methodology follows a standard LLM synthesis pipeline.
- **Experimental Thoroughness**: ⭐⭐⭐ Workshop paper with only dataset description and human evaluation scores; no model training validation.
- **Writing Quality**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Well-structured but limited in technical depth.
- **Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Fills a gap in first aid NLP datasets, though safety concerns limit practical deployment.
