# Foundation Model Priors Enhance Object Focus in Feature Space for Source-Free Object Detection

**Conference**: CVPR2026
**arXiv**: [2512.17514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17514)
**Code**: To be confirmed
**Area**: Object Detection
**Keywords**: Source-Free Object Detection, Foundation Model Priors, Feature Space Regularization, Noise-Robust Pseudo Labels, Mean-Teacher

## TL;DR

This paper proposes FALCON-SFOD, a framework that leverages class-agnostic binary masks generated by a foundation model (OV-SAM) to regularize the detector's feature space via Spatial Prior-Aware Regularization (SPAR), and introduces an Imbalance-aware Robust Pseudo Label loss (IRPL) to achieve object-focused representations in source-free object detection, attaining state-of-the-art results across multiple benchmarks.

## Background & Motivation

1. **Source-Free Object Detection (SFOD)** aims to adapt a source-trained detector to an unlabeled target domain without access to source data, making it highly practical for applications such as autonomous driving and surveillance.
2. **Limitations of Prior Work**: Current SOTA methods (IRG, PETS, Simple-SFOD, DRU) all adopt the Mean-Teacher self-labeling framework but focus primarily on **pseudo-label filtering/refinement**, neglecting fundamental issues at the feature level.
3. **Domain Shift Weakens Object Focus**: The authors identify that domain shift causes feature activations to diffuse from foreground objects into background clutter, making channel-mean activation maps spatially dispersed with blurred object boundaries—this is the **root cause** of degraded pseudo-label quality.
4. **Neglected Feature-Level Issues**: Existing works have largely overlooked how to enhance structured, object-centric representations in the feature space.
5. **Potential of Foundation Models**: Large-scale visual foundation models (e.g., DINOv2, SAM) exhibit strong cross-domain generalization, but online usage incurs significant computational overhead (e.g., DINO Teacher requires online alignment).
6. **Severe Foreground-Background Imbalance**: Detection inherently involves scarce positive samples and abundant background regions; combined with class label noise from the teacher network, this renders training highly unstable.

## Method

### Overall Architecture: FALCON-SFOD

Two complementary modules are coupled to the standard Mean-Teacher framework: **SPAR** (Spatial Prior-Aware Regularization) and **IRPL** (Imbalance-aware Robust Pseudo Label loss), trained end-to-end together with standard localization losses.

### SPAR — Spatial Prior-Aware Regularization

- **Offline Preprocessing**: A frozen OV-SAM model performs a single forward pass over target domain images to produce class-agnostic binary foreground masks $A_G$ (discarding category information; any segmented region = 1, otherwise = 0).
- **Online Regularization**: The channel-wise mean activation $A_S$ of the student backbone's last-layer features is resized to match the mask resolution and aligned to $A_G$ via a combined **L1 + Dice** loss:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{SPAR}} = \frac{\lambda_1}{H'W'}\sum_{j,k}|A_S[j,k] - A_G[j,k]| + \lambda_2 \cdot \text{DiceLoss}(A_S, A_G)$$
- **Zero Inference Overhead**: The foundation model is invoked only once during preprocessing and is not used during training or inference.
- Hyperparameters: $\lambda_1=1, \lambda_2=2$.

### IRPL — Imbalance-aware Robust Pseudo Label Loss

- **Peak-Adjust Transform**: A margin $m$ is added to the peak of the student prediction probability $\mathbf{p}$ before normalization:
    - Teacher–student agreement ($\hat{c}=t$): gradients are suppressed by $p_{\hat{c}}/(p_{\hat{c}}+m) \ll 1$, providing a built-in soft early-stopping effect to prevent overfitting to already-correct labels.
    - Teacher–student disagreement ($\hat{c} \neq t$): gradients retain the standard CE form, preserving full corrective signal against erroneous teacher labels.
- **Foreground-Background Weighting** $w_{\hat{c}}$: Mitigates the inherent positive-negative imbalance in detection.
- **KL Divergence Entropy Regularization**: Aligns the mean class distribution across all proposals with a uniform distribution to suppress head-class dominance.
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{IRPL}} = \sum_{(\hat{b},\hat{c})} w_{\hat{c}}[\alpha(-\log p'_{\hat{c}}) + \beta(1-p_{\hat{c}})] + \gamma D_{\text{KL}}(\bar{\mathbf{p}} \| \mathcal{U}_K)$$

### Theoretical Analysis

- **Theorem 1**: Under the standard Mean-Teacher framework, the detection risk upper bound contains a $1/\lambda$ multiplicative inflation of the classification term plus a localization bias $\eta_{\text{reg}}$ and a miss-detection rate $2\zeta$.
- **Theorem 2**: IRPL replaces the multiplicative inflation $1/\lambda$ with an additive term $2\delta + 2w\delta/a$ (arbitrarily tight as $\delta \to 0$), yielding a strictly tighter bound.
- SPAR directly reduces $\eta_{\text{reg}}$ and $\zeta$ by cleaning feature activations.

## Key Experimental Results

### Main Results (4 Domain Shift Scenarios)

| Scenario | Method | mAP/AP |
|------|------|--------|
| Cityscapes→Foggy (C→F) | Simple-SFOD | 45.0 |
| C→F | DRU | 43.7 |
| **C→F** | **FALCON-SFOD** | **46.9 (+1.9)** |
| Sim10k→Cityscapes (S→C) | Simple-SFOD | 55.4 |
| **S→C** | **FALCON-SFOD** | **58.8 (+3.4)** |
| KITTI→Cityscapes (K→C) | DRU | 45.1 |
| **K→C** | **FALCON-SFOD** | **50.1 (+5.0)** |
| Cityscapes→BDD100k | Simple-SFOD | 34.3 |
| **C→BDD100k** | **FALCON-SFOD** | **36.9 (+2.6)** |

### Extreme Domain Shift Experiments

| Scenario | Baseline | FALCON-SFOD | Δ |
|------|----------|-------------|---|
| PascalVOC→Clipart (artistic style) | 33.6 | 35.5 | +1.9 |
| FLIR Visible→Infrared (thermal) | 56.7 | 58.5 | +1.8 |
| FLIR Infrared→COCO (thermal to RGB) | 19.4 | 20.9 | +1.5 |

### Ablation Study

| Component | C→F mAP | S→C AP |
|------|---------|--------|
| Baseline | 45.0 | 55.4 |
| + SPAR | 46.1 | 57.5 |
| + IRPL | 45.8 | 56.8 |
| + SPAR + IRPL (full) | **46.9** | **58.8** |

**Long-tail Category Analysis**: Gains concentrate on low-frequency classes (train +4.1, truck +4.0, bus +2.9), with Pearson correlation $r_\Delta = -0.90$, and no degradation on head classes.

**Mask Source Ablation**: OV-SAM > ESC-Net > GSAM > Source maps, validating that stronger foundation models yield better spatial priors.

## Highlights & Insights

- **Insightful Problem Identification**: This work is the first to identify and demonstrate the degradation of object-focused representations in the SFOD feature space, addressing the problem at the feature level rather than the pseudo-label level.
- **Lightweight and Elegant Design**: The foundation model is used only offline once, incurring zero additional overhead during training and inference.
- **Solid Theoretical Grounding**: A complete detection risk decomposition and upper-bound analysis is provided, with SPAR and IRPL each corresponding to specific terms in the theoretical framework.
- **Long-tail Friendly**: IRPL substantially improves performance on low-frequency classes (train/truck/bus) without harming head classes.

## Limitations & Future Work

- Performance of SPAR depends on OV-SAM mask quality; degraded segmentation in extreme domains (e.g., thermal infrared) may reduce its effectiveness.
- Classification accuracy gains are relatively modest (AP for person/car classes is nearly unchanged), with primary benefits concentrated on rare categories.
- Validation is limited to the Faster R-CNN family (single-stage comparison involves only SF-YOLO); the approach has not been extended to Transformer-based detectors such as DETR.
- The theoretical assumption of a diagonal lower bound $\lambda > 0$ on the transition matrix $T$ may not hold under extreme domain shifts.
- The end-to-end adaptation stage requires tuning multiple hyperparameters ($\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \alpha, \beta, \gamma, m$); sensitivity analysis is relegated to supplementary material.
- The L1+Dice combination for SPAR is simple and effective, but finer-grained region-level alignment or multi-scale feature regularization remains unexplored.
- On BDD100k, mAP of 36.9% still lags behind the UDAOD method MTM (37.3%), indicating that a performance ceiling persists under the source-free setting.
